# Bolt and the Dark Lighthouse

## Controls
 Action -Keys 

Move - Arrow keys / A, D 
Jump (hold for higher) - Space / Z / Up / W 
Run - Shift / X 
Advance dialogue - Space / Enter 
Skip cutscene - Esc 



## Story
The town of Cogsworth runs on the Great Lighthouse's **Sun Core**. The **Rust Baron**
steals it and leaves his Rustbugs behind to stop anyone from following him. **Bolt**,
a small repair robot, chases him to his outpost at the end of 1-1. In the ending, Bolt
takes the core back and relights the lighthouse. The Baron turns out to have stolen it
because his outpost was always cold and dark, and Bolt invites him to share the light.

## What maps to what
Original - This game 
Mario - Bolt (small / big)
Goomba - Rustbug 
Koopa - Shellbot: stomp it into a shell, then kick the shell  
Coin - Gear (100 gears = 1 extra life)
Mushroom - Battery: Bolt grows, can take one hit and can break bricks 
1-Up - Spare Chip (from the hidden block above the 4th pipe gap) 
Star - Overcharge: 10 seconds of invincibility 
Flagpole / castle - Signal antenna / Rust Baron's outpost
